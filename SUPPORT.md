## Questions, Bug Reports and Feature Requests

Open an issue using the appropriate template:

- [Bug report](https://github.com/innovation-ways/iw-jobs-radar/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)
- [Feature request](https://github.com/innovation-ways/iw-jobs-radar/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml)

## Response Expectations

Jobs Radar is a free personal project, maintained on a best-effort basis in the maintainer's spare
time. There is no paid version, no hosted service and no support contract. Replies may be slow, and
a feature request may not be taken up; forks are welcome under the Apache-2.0 licence.
